Cruise Terms Guide

Ship Navigation

Bow: Front of the ship

Stern: Back of the ship

Port: Left side (facing forward)

Starboard: Right side (facing forward)

Cabin Categories

Interior: No windows, most affordable

Ocean View: Window or porthole

Balcony: Private balcony access

Suite: Spacious with living areas

Cruise Experience

Shore Excursion: Organized port tours

Sea Day: Day at sea, no port stops

Tender: Boat to shore when can't dock

Lido Deck: Main pool and activity area
